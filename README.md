# LOVE HEART ❤️
### A Little Creative Coding Experiment

> Got bored, felt like making something. So here we are. ❤️

A small digital artwork built around a simple idea: bring words of love together and let them form a heart.

This project combines mathematical heart geometry, multilingual typography, motion, and a subtle heartbeat effect into one animated visual.

## The artwork

![LOVE HEART animation](media/LOVE_HEART.gif)

## The idea behind it

No grand product pitch. No complicated story.

Just a little experiment in turning code into something expressive — and sharing the result in case it makes somebody smile or comes in handy.

## What went into it

- **Mathematical geometry** — a parametric heart shape defines the composition.
- **Generative typography** — phrases meaning “I love you” in multiple languages fill the heart.
- **Animation** — the phrases move into place to build the final shape.
- **Atmosphere** — a starfield and shooting stars add a little motion to the scene.
- **Heartbeat effect** — the completed heart gently pulses.
